    • Educational Programs at English Kindergarten

    Customized programs for every stage of child development

    At English Kindergarten, we have developed unique educational programs tailored to every stage of child development, from infancy to mandatory kindergarten age. Each program combines natural English learning with diverse activities that encourage harmonious development in all areas - cognitive, social, emotional, and motor. Our professional staff, composed of certified educators who are native English speakers, provides each child with personal attention and a warm, challenging environment.

    Our private kindergarten network offers a safe, clean, and inspiring environment with advanced educational equipment and shaded playgrounds. We emphasize creating a warm and personal connection with each family, and regularly update parents on their children's development and growth.

    Nursery - Ages 3 months to 1 year

    Our infant program focuses on creating a safe, nurturing, and enriching environment that allows babies to develop at their own pace. Our educators provide personal and loving care to each infant, while providing initial exposure to English language sounds through songs, gentle conversations, and sensory games.

    The program includes age-appropriate activities such as infant massage, motor games, tummy time, and sensory activities that encourage motor and sensory development. We place great emphasis on a stable and consistent routine, giving the infant a sense of security and calm.

    Program highlights:

    • Personal ratio of 1:3 (educator to infants)
    • Daily exposure to English through songs and conversations
    • Diverse sensory and motor activities
    • Stable and consistent routine
    • Healthy and balanced nutrition
    • Daily updates to parents

    Toddler Garden - Ages 1 to 2 years

    At this age, children begin to develop independence, curiosity, and initial social skills. Our toddler program focuses on encouraging independent exploration, developing communication skills in English, and learning through play. Our educators create an inspiring environment that allows toddlers to discover, learn, and grow.

    The program includes diverse activities such as English story time, singing and movement, arts and crafts, free and structured play, and outdoor activities. We encourage the development of gross and fine motor skills, as well as social skills such as cooperation, taking turns, and expressing emotions.

    Program highlights:

    • Learning English through play and experience
    • Development of social and emotional skills
    • Encouraging independence and initiative
    • Creative and artistic activities
    • Free and structured play time
    • Daily outdoor activities

    Kindergarten - Ages 2-4 years

    This is the central stage in the English Kindergarten program, where children develop advanced language skills, complex social skills, and higher cognitive abilities. Our kindergarten program combines structured learning with free play, preparing children for first grade while maintaining a fun and playful atmosphere.

    Children are exposed to a wide range of topics and concepts in English, including letters, numbers, colors, shapes, natural science, and more. The program includes extended story time, thematic projects, mathematical and scientific activities, arts and crafts, music and movement, and group games that encourage cooperation.

    Program highlights:

    • Advanced proficiency in English
    • First grade preparation - writing, reading, mathematics
    • Development of thinking skills and problem solving
    • Fascinating thematic projects
    • Art, music and movement activities
    • Educational trips and tours

    Additional Programs and Special Services

    English Summer Camp

    A special summer program with fun and engaging activities in English - water games, crafts, cooking, trips, and more. Suitable for ages 2-6.

    Extended Daycare

    Daycare service until 5:00 PM with hot lunch, rest, and enrichment activities. Also available for mandatory kindergarten children.

    Integration of Children with Special Needs

    We believe in equal education and accept children with special needs. Our staff receives special training and provides individual support.

    Parent Support and Guidance

    Regular parent meetings, personal counseling, and parenting workshops. We see parents as full partners in the educational process.

    Pricing and Programs

    We offer a variety of flexible programs tailored to each family's needs. Prices vary according to the child's age, chosen program, and hours of stay. You can choose between a full program (5 days a week), partial program (3 days), or individual days. Contact us for a personalized quote.
