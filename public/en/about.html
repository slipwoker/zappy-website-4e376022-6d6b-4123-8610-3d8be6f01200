    • The Story of גן אנגלית

    A journey of education, love and commitment to children

    גן אנגלית was founded from a deep belief in the power of quality education to shape our children's future. As parents ourselves, we searched for a place where our children could grow, learn and develop in a warm, nurturing and challenging environment. When we couldn't find the perfect place, we decided to create it ourselves.

    The idea behind גן אנגלית was simple yet revolutionary - to create a network of private kindergartens that combines quality bilingual education with an advanced educational approach that respects each child's uniqueness. We knew that the first years of a child's life are critical to their development, and we wanted to give parents the opportunity to provide their children with the best foundation for the future.

    Today, after years of dedicated work, גן אנגלית has become the leading network in bilingual early childhood education. We are proud of the hundreds of families who chose us, and the thousands of children who grew and developed in our frameworks. Every day we continue to operate from the same passion and commitment that led us from the beginning - to give every child the tools, love and support they need to succeed.

    Our Educational Approach

    The educational approach of גן אנגלית is based on current research in child development and language acquisition. We believe that children learn best through play, experience, and independent exploration. Therefore, our educational program is built around these principles, while integrating natural and organic English learning.

    We use the Total Physical Response (TPR) method for English learning, which combines movement, music, and play. Children are exposed to the English language in meaningful and relevant contexts to their lives, enabling natural language acquisition without pressure or coercion.

    Additionally, we place great emphasis on developing social and emotional skills. Children learn to identify and express emotions, resolve conflicts peacefully, cooperate, and consider others. We encourage independence, initiative, and responsibility, and provide children with opportunities to make decisions and solve problems.
